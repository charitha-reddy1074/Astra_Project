Access Provisioning Records
Record Period: November 2024
System: ServiceNow ITSM — IAM Module
Maintained By: IT Operations — Access Management Team
Classification: INTERNAL

Purpose
This document records all access provisioning and de-provisioning activities performed during the reporting period. Each record includes the ticket reference, requester, access granted/revoked, approval chain, and completion status to support audit and compliance requirements.

Provisioning Log

Process Notes
All provisioning requests are initiated via ServiceNow self-service portal or HR system integration
Manager approval required for all standard access requests
CISO approval required for privileged and sensitive data access
Offboarding requests are auto-triggered by HR system upon termination entry
Vendor access requires dual approval: Procurement and Data Owner
All provisioning activities are logged with timestamp and approver identity
