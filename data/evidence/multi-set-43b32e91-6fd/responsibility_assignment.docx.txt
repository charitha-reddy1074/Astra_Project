Vulnerability Management Program
Responsibility Assignment Document

Document Details

Security Team Responsibilities

Perform authenticated and unauthenticated vulnerability scans across all asset classes
Review and triage scan findings within 48 hours of scan completion
Generate weekly and monthly vulnerability management reports
Maintain and update the vulnerability tracking register
Coordinate remediation activities with asset owners and infrastructure teams

Infrastructure Team Responsibilities

Patch operating systems in accordance with defined SLA timelines
Validate remediation effectiveness post-patching
Report patch deployment status to the Vulnerability Management team
Maintain patch deployment logs for audit purposes

Application Owners

Remediate application-layer vulnerabilities within agreed SLAs
Submit and document exception requests through the approved process
Confirm remediation completion and provide evidence to Security team
Maintain application inventory in the asset register
