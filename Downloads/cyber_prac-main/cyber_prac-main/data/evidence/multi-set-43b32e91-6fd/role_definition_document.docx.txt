2. Purpose & Scope

This document defines all access roles within the enterprise identity management framework, including role descriptions, entitlements, assignment criteria, and review requirements. All roles must be formally assigned through the Access Granting Procedure (IAM-PROC-2026-003).
Scope: All employees, contractors, service accounts, and privileged users accessing corporate systems, cloud platforms, and data assets.

4. Role Assignment Principles

Least Privilege: Users are granted only the minimum access required for their job function
Need to Know: Access to sensitive data requires documented business justification
Separation of Duties: Conflicting roles (e.g. Finance Analyst + Finance Approver) may not be held by the same individual
Role-Based Assignment: Access is granted to roles, not individuals, wherever technically feasible
Privileged Access: All privileged roles require just-in-time access via Azure PIM and phishing-resistant MFA
Service Accounts: Must be approved by a named human owner and reviewed monthly
